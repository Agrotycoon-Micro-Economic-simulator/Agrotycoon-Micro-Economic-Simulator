## Source and scope

The PDF contains provincial producer-price tables, usually for 2020–2024, and shows the Indonesian national average as the final row. The official publication says its producer-price data are sourced from BPS. Table references below use the printed page numbers shown in the PDF's table of contents. A table's existence is not proof that every province has a value; dash values mean unavailable, not zero.

## Matching policy and next work

1. Provincial values can support regional price variation only when the owner has selected a region and the product form matches.
2. These are producer prices. Do not substitute them for consumer retail prices, downstream processing prices, export prices, or net revenue.
3. No price-only observation is sufficient to approve a runtime value. Pair with output yield, production cycle, input costs, labor, loss, capital/fixed cost, transport, and stage-specific price.
4. Do not infer a missing provincial value from national average, neighboring provinces, or an adjacent product category.
5. Do not use generic “lada” for both white and black pepper; do not use Robusta for Arabica; do not use Pisang Ambon for Cavendish; do not use live chicken for carcass meat; do not use live duck for duck eggs; do not use thick rubber for liquid latex without a conversion study.
6. No runtime values or IDs are changed by this research checkpoint. Tests are not applicable to these documentation-only changes and were not run.
