## Engine contract — generic transformations

Every transformation recipe must identify its input commodity IDs and units, quantity per batch, all outputs and their roles (primary/by-product/waste), output quantity per batch, required process-cost lines, batch capacity when known, and evidence for each numeric market price or cost. Null means unknown, not free or zero. Saleable by-products require a price record with source, period, geography and evidence kind. Unsafe or unmarketable residues remain non-saleable waste. The engine may plan known physical quantities while marking economics incomplete; it must not permit execution when required quantities, output quantities, stock, costs, or provenance are incomplete.
